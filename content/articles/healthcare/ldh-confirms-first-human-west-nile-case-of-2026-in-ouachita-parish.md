The Louisiana Department of Health confirmed on July 17 the state's first human West Nile virus case of the 2026 season, in Ouachita Parish. The virus has also been detected in mosquito samples from 13 parishes.

## An unusually early season

State officials said it was the worst start to a West Nile season since 2004, and WAFB noted that federal health officials described 2026 as the earliest and most active start in more than two decades nationally.

## Last year for comparison

Louisiana recorded 86 confirmed human West Nile cases in 2025, including four deaths, according to the figures cited in coverage.

## Protecting yourself

The health department's standard advice is to use EPA-registered repellent, wear long sleeves at dawn and dusk, and drain standing water around homes, where mosquitoes breed. The sources reviewed do not include a later statewide 2026 case count.
