University Medical Center New Orleans planned a Community Health Fair & Fall Fest for October 17, according to a regional healthcare newsletter. The event was described as free for the public.

## Why community health fairs matter

Health fairs commonly offer blood pressure checks, screenings and information on local services. The listing reviewed for this article does not itemize which screenings or vendors were included, so residents should rely on UMC's own channels for specifics.

## Context

UMC is part of the LCMC Health system and anchors the medical district along Tulane Avenue in New Orleans.
