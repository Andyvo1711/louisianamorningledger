The federal agency overseeing Medicare extended the termination deadline for Northern Louisiana Medical Center in Ruston, according to Becker's Hospital Review. The original date was August 30.

## The new timeline

An August 18 CMS letter required the hospital to submit an acceptable plan of correction by September 2. It also gave the hospital until October 1 to correct patient-care deficiencies or lose Medicare funding.

## Stakes

Becker's reported that termination on October 1 would cut off reimbursement, could threaten the hospital's state licensure and would likely lead to closure. The hospital is a 130-bed facility operated by Allegiance Health Management.
