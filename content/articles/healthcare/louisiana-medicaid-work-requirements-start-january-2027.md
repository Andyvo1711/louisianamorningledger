Medicaid work requirements under a federal law passed in July 2025 will take effect in Louisiana on January 1, 2027. The Louisiana Department of Health says most members will not be affected, and a September 2026 report citing state data estimated that up to 58,000 recipients might have to take additional steps to comply.

## Who is exempt

The state's fact sheet lists exemptions that include Indian Health Service members, people already meeting TANF or SNAP work requirements, caregivers of a dependent child 13 or younger or of a disabled individual, disabled veterans and medically frail individuals. About 1.1 million enrollees will be automatically exempt.

## Timeline

For 2027 renewals, the state will check compliance for at least one month in the six months before a member's renewal begins. Members up for renewal in January 2027 were to receive a notice in May 2026 and a renewal packet with work requirement materials in November 2026. A verification form was issued on September 25. Proposed rules were published in July with public comment in August, and the sources reviewed do not confirm final adoption.

## Risk of coverage loss

LDH warns that failure to respond to requests from Louisiana Medicaid could close coverage even if a member remains eligible. Members can call 888-342-6207 or visit ldh.la.gov/medicaid/work-requirements.
