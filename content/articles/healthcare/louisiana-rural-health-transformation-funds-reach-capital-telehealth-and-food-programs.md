Louisiana's Rural Health Transformation Program moved from announcement to application season this summer. The Louisiana Department of Health said in December 2025 that the state would receive more than $208 million through the federal program, among the highest awards in the nation, and a January 2026 presentation listed $208,374,448 for year one.

## Funding notices

LDH has issued notices of funding opportunity for several initiatives, including a Capital Improvement Program and a telehealth infrastructure program, a Rural Clinician Credit Bank, collaborative provider models, a Rural Medicaid Alternative Payment Model (a revised notice dated June 29) and a Food is Medicine program (notice dated July 2) that lists rural hospitals among eligible applicants.

## What is still unclear

The sources reviewed contain no published list of hospitals receiving awards and no final award amounts for the capital or telehealth programs. An LDH advisory council is also scheduled to meet on the program, according to a state agenda document.

*Sources: [LDH announcement](https://ldh.la.gov/news/RHTP-funding-announcement), [LDH Food as Medicine notice](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/docs/Secretary/RHTP/RHTP-Food-as-Medicine_NOFO_2July2026.pdf), [LDH capital improvement notice](https://ldh.la.gov/assets/docs/Secretary/RHTP/CapitalNOFO_Final_1June2026.pdf).*
