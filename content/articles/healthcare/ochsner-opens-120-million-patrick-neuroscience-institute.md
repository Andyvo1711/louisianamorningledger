Ochsner Health has opened The Debra H. and Robert J. Patrick Neuroscience Institute, a $120 million, 132,000-square-foot facility on its Jefferson Highway campus in New Orleans. Photos from the opening ceremony are dated October 1, and clinic appointments began in October.

## Four clinical pillars

The institute is built around neurology, neurosurgery, physical medicine and rehabilitation, and behavioral health. It also offers integrative therapies, including music therapy, yoga and creative writing. Ochsner describes it as the only neuroscience facility of its kind between Houston and Atlanta and the largest location for its systemwide neuroscience program.

## Gifts and technology

A transformational gift from Debbie and Bobby Patrick made the project possible, with support from other donors. The Patricks also gave a separate gift to establish the Patrick Neuroscience Academy within the institute. The building houses the Team Gleason Assistive Technology Lab, which gives people with ALS and other neurological conditions access to communication tools.

Earlier announcements said the center would open in late 2026; later coverage points to October.
