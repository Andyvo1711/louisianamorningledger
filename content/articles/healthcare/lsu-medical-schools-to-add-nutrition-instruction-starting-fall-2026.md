Both of LSU's medical schools agreed to provide at least 40 hours of nutrition instruction, or equivalent competency, beginning in the fall of 2026, according to an LSU feature published in April on how future doctors are trained to treat root causes of disease.

## Why nutrition

Louisiana ranks among the states with the highest obesity rates; the Trust for America's Health reported that 39.2% of adults were obese in 2024. LSU's Pennington Biomedical Research Center has also launched studies on rural health and on children's nutrition and activity.

## Where it fits

The new requirement lands as LSU consolidates its health sciences and research campuses under a single flagship structure effective August 1. The sources reviewed do not describe how the curriculum will be delivered or assessed.
