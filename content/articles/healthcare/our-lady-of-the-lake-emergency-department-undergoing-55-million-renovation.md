WAFB reported on September 8 that the emergency department at Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center in Baton Rouge is undergoing a $55 million renovation. FMOL Health first announced the project in 2025, and later updates described construction as progressing.

## About the hospital

Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center is an 800-bed hospital and a major emergency destination for the Baton Rouge area.

## What patients can expect

The reports reviewed describe a renovation of the existing emergency department rather than a new building. They do not give a final completion date or detail how many treatment bays will be added, so patients should watch for updates from the hospital as phases open.
