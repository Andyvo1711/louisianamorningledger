Trust for America's Health released its annual State of Obesity report on September 24, finding that adult obesity rates were largely unchanged in 2025, with 21 states at or above 35%. For the first time since 2020, no state was at or above 40%.

## Louisiana's position

The previous year's report found Louisiana's adult obesity rate was 39.2% in 2024, up from 35.9% in 2019. The sources reviewed do not confirm Louisiana's 2025 figure, which appears in the new report's state fact sheet.

## What is being done locally

State programs include the SNAP food restriction waiver that took effect February 18, barring purchases of soft drinks, energy drinks and candy with benefits, and LSU's Pennington Biomedical Research Center, which is running studies on rural health and children's nutrition.

Readers who want the current Louisiana number should check TFAH's state fact sheet at tfah.org.
