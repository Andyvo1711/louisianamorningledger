Local leaders in north Louisiana reacted in early July to a federal inspection report on Northern Louisiana Medical Center (NLMC), the 130-bed hospital in Ruston operated by Allegiance Health Management. WAFB reported that the report found two dozen alleged violations.

## What the survey found

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services and the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services issued an 87-page report that highlighted 24 deficiencies and reviewed 32 patient cases. WAFB's June 30 coverage was headlined with allegations of missing nurses, dirty rooms and broken equipment.

## Medicare at stake

The Louisiana Department of Health said the hospital did not qualify as a Medicare provider of services. A June 15 letter set August 30 as the termination date if the problems were not fixed, which would cut off Medicare reimbursement.

## Local reaction

KNOE requested the hospital's plan of correction, but both the hospital and the state health department declined to release it. State Sen. Jay Morris said removing the operator could be a lengthy legal process.
