David Callecod, MBA, FACHE, has been named chief executive officer of Ochsner LSU Health System of North Louisiana, according to a news item on Bossier Press dated September 2. He had served as interim CEO since November 2024.

## A system in transition

The Shreveport-Bossier system runs hospitals and clinics that serve north Louisiana and east Texas. Leadership continuity matters as the region sees growth in health care demand and large employer investment.

## What the announcement does not say

The item reviewed for this story does not list strategic priorities for the system, and the page's date may be a display date, so the exact date of the decision should be checked against Ochsner's newsroom.
