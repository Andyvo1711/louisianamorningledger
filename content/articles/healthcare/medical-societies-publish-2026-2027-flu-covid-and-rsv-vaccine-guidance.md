Louisiana families heading into respiratory-virus season are getting vaccine guidance from medical societies, because the CDC has not yet issued its 2026-27 recommendations. WAFB reported on October 2 that experts say now is an ideal time to protect against flu.

## What the societies recommend

Four medical groups published 2026-27 guidance. For flu, they advise everyone aged 6 months and older without contraindications to get vaccinated. For COVID, they recommend vaccination for all adults, children aged 6 to 23 months, and pregnant or breastfeeding women. RSV vaccines are recommended mainly for newborns, pregnant women and adults 75 and older, with some groups adding at-risk adults 50 to 74.

## Practical notes

Flu vaccines began shipping in August, with COVID vaccines expected in September or October. Medicare covers the fall shots, and most private insurers say they will continue coverage through 2027. A new mRNA flu vaccine is also available for adults 50 and older.

The sources reviewed contain no Louisiana-specific case counts for the season.
