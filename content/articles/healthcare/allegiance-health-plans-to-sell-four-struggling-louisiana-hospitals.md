Allegiance Health Management plans to sell four financially struggling rural Louisiana hospitals at the direction of the Louisiana Department of Health, according to an October 7 broadcast investigation. Two of the hospitals are in Acadiana: Mercy Regional Medical Center in Ville Platte and Acadian Medical Center in Eunice.

## Problems described

The reports describe unpaid payroll taxes, broken equipment, overdue bills, the loss of doctors and staff, and a shortage of critical supplies.

## History

Allegiance acquired Mercy Regional, Acadian Medical Center and Minden Medical Center from LifePoint Health in a deal that closed July 31, 2018, growing its system to 16 hospitals in Louisiana, Texas, Mississippi and Arkansas.

## What remains unknown

The reports reviewed do not name a buyer, price or timeline. Allegiance also operates Northern Louisiana Medical Center in Ruston, which faced federal Medicare termination warnings earlier this year.
