Monica Adams resigned as chief executive of Northern Louisiana Medical Center on August 21 after less than three months in the job, the hospital said. The Lincoln Parish Journal and Becker's Hospital Review both reported the resignation.

## Interim leadership

Chief Operating Officer Arnie Young was named interim CEO, effective immediately. The hospital said Adams cited personal and family reasons.

## A hospital under federal review

The change came as the 130-bed Ruston hospital faced a federal deadline to correct patient-care deficiencies or lose Medicare funding. The facility is operated by Allegiance Health Management.
