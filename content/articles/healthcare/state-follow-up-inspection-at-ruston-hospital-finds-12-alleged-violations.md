A state inspection of Northern Louisiana Medical Center in Ruston on July 29 found 12 alleged violations, according to KNOE reporting. That compares with 24 deficiencies in the original June survey. One report mentions 14 findings in an earlier July follow-up, so the counts differ by source.

## Examples from the findings

The findings included expired atropine dated July 2023 in a dusty container, suction regulators last serviced in 2012 and 2013, and a CT scanner that had been out of service since December 2024.

## Why it matters

The hospital, a 130-bed facility operated by Allegiance Health Management, was working against federal deadlines to keep its Medicare participation. The drop in the count was notable, but regulators were still weighing whether the remaining problems were serious enough to threaten its status.
