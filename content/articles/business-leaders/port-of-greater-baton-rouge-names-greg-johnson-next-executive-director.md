The Port of Greater Baton Rouge's board of commissioners has chosen Greg Johnson, the port's deputy director, as its next executive director, WBRZ reported on September 9.

## A long-serving predecessor

Hardman, who has led the port since 2006, announced his decision at a board meeting. The board named Johnson, another longtime member of the port's management team, to serve as the next executive director.

## Why the port matters

The Port of Greater Baton Rouge handles cargo on the Mississippi River in a corridor that includes some of the state's largest petrochemical and industrial employers. A leadership change of this kind generally signals continuity, since the successor is an internal candidate who has worked alongside the current director.

The report reviewed for this article does not give a start date for the transition.
