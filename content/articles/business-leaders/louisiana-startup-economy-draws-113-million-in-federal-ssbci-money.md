Louisiana has $113 million in federal money to build out its startup economy, according to an August Technical.ly report, which argues the state is not itself selecting which startups receive it.

## Who is investing

Eight funds are already investing the dollars, including Boot64, Tulane Ventures, Ochsner Ventures, Propeller, The Idea Village's Momentum Fund and 1834 Ventures. The state says the roster is still growing.

## Local ecosystem

The Idea Village's Momentum Fund is a $7 million venture fund backed by 27 investors that launched in 2023 and supports alumni of its VILLAGEx accelerator. Louisiana Economic Development also lists nearly a dozen small business programs, and Technical.ly published an April 2026 guide to accelerators and incubators.

The report reviewed does not detail the dollar amounts deployed so far.
