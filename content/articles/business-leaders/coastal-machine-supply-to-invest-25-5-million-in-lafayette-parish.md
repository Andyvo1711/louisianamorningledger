Coastal Machine & Supply Inc., a Medora Manufacturing company, announced on September 3 that it will invest $25.5 million to expand its Lafayette Parish operations, according to KEDM and Louisiana Economic Development.

## Jobs

The company is expected to create 129 direct new jobs while retaining 58 current positions. Trade publications say the project may produce 240 total jobs when indirect positions are counted.

## A growing local cluster

The state's release ties the expansion to what it calls Acadiana's growing space economy, a link that also appears in coverage of SpaceX's planned $100 billion launch complex in Vermilion Parish, announced weeks earlier.

Details such as wage levels and the timeline for hiring were not included in the summaries reviewed.
