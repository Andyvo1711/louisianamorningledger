Katoen Natie broke ground on a $25 million expansion at its Baton Rouge Polymers Terminal, with construction expected to be completed by October 2026 and operations beginning the following month, according to Louisiana Economic Development.

## Jobs

The project is expected to create 13 direct new jobs while retaining 29 current positions in East Baton Rouge Parish. Biz New Orleans also covered the company's plan.

## What to watch

If the schedule holds, operations would start in November. The sources reviewed do not confirm that completion has occurred.
