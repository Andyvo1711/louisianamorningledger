Hyundai Steel and POSCO held a ceremonial groundbreaking in early September for their $5.8 billion steel mill in Ascension Parish near Donaldsonville, WAFB reported on September 4. SteelOrbis also reported the groundbreaking.

## Timeline

Full construction is set to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026, with output starting in early 2029. The original 2025 announcement had said construction would start in the third quarter, so the schedule has moved. WAFB reported that the Hyundai-POSCO venture had not yet received the air permit required before actual construction can begin.

## Scale

The mill is a joint venture planned to produce 2.7 million metric tons of hot rolled, cold rolled and galvanized steel annually. Louisiana Economic Development has said the project remains on track, while Hyundai has said it is monitoring tariffs.

## Opposition

Some residents and advocacy groups remain opposed, as a June Louisiana Illuminator report on "hopes and fears" in Donaldsonville described.
