Black Bayou Energy Hub announced a $1.6 billion Louisiana expansion along the state's growing LNG corridor, with an Area Development listing dated September 14. The company is headquartered in Lafayette and will develop natural gas infrastructure in Cameron Parish.

## Timeline and jobs

Construction is expected to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026, with commercial operations anticipated in late 2028. Sources differ on employment: Area Development lists 23 jobs, while another source describes 58 potential new job opportunities across the Southwest and Acadiana regions. The figures may measure direct versus total positions, though the reports do not say so.

## Local partners

The Southwest Louisiana Economic Development Alliance and the Lafayette Economic Development Authority are quoted in coverage of the project, which they describe as a shared regional win.
