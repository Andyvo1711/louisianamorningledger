Leading Edge Manufacturing, a metal fabricator, is expanding in Youngsville and Broussard, according to an Area Development listing dated September 27. The project is expected to create 20 jobs in Lafayette Parish.

## Small-scale growth in a busy corridor

While the expansion is modest compared with multibillion-dollar announcements elsewhere in Louisiana, it reflects steady demand for fabrication work in Acadiana, where energy, marine and aerospace suppliers cluster around Lafayette.

The listing reviewed for this article does not state the investment amount, wage levels or construction schedule. Readers can check Louisiana Economic Development for a formal project announcement.
