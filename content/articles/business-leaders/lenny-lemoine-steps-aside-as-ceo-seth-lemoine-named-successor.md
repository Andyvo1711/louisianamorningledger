LEMOINE, the Louisiana-founded construction and services company, announced an executive leadership transition in mid-2026. Lenny Lemoine, who served as CEO for more than 25 years, is moving into a new role as executive chairman.

## The successor

The company's board selected Seth Lemoine, a 24-year veteran of the firm, as CEO after a formal search and selection process. He most recently served as chief operating officer of the company's response and resiliency service line. William Lemoine will move into the COO position.

## Growth under the outgoing CEO

According to Technical.ly and WBRZ coverage, the business grew under Lenny Lemoine from a regional commercial general contractor and construction management firm into a national, comprehensive services provider with more than 1,100 employees.

Sources differ on the company's headquarters, describing it as based in Lafayette or in Baton Rouge, so this article does not specify a city.
