Entergy reported third-quarter 2026 results on November 2, with adjusted earnings of $1.98 per share compared with $1.53 a year earlier and above the Zacks consensus of $1.71.

## What drove the numbers

Industrial sales rose 4.9% to 17,055 gigawatt-hours, which the company tied to growth from data center and primary metals customers. Entergy narrowed its 2026 adjusted earnings guidance to a range of $4.35 to $4.45 per share. Management cited data-center interest in the range of 7 to 12 gigawatts and another 3 to 5 gigawatts of traditional industrial discussions.

## Louisiana and beyond

Entergy also expanded its Google agreement for a data center campus in West Memphis, Arkansas, which is outside Louisiana. Entergy Louisiana issued $1 billion in bonds with 10-year and 30-year maturities at an average rate below 6%.

The company is also building power plants tied to Meta's Richland Parish project.
