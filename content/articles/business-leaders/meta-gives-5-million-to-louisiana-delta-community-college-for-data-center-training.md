Louisiana Delta Community College announced a historic $5 million donation from Meta on July 13 to create scholarships that train local residents for data center jobs tied to the company's Richland Parish campus.

## Who qualifies

Scholarships cover any data center-related trade certificate or course the college offers. One report says that, beginning with the class of 2026, Richland Parish high school graduates will be eligible for full scholarships for these programs. Fields include data center operations, construction trades, electrical work, fiber optics and HVAC. The college said application details would come later.

## Early results

WWNO reported in September that 40 people had graduated from Delta's new four-week data center technician program since November, with Meta and its contractors helping the school buy equipment and train instructors.

Meta is expanding the site to 5 gigawatts of compute capacity, and the data center is expected to provide more than 1,000 roles once operational.
