Bollinger Shipyards quietly began cutting steel in April for the first U.S. Coast Guard Arctic Security Cutter, ahead of the finalized contract award, USNI News reported on July 2.

## The contract

Bollinger announced on December 29, 2025 that it had signed a contract for four Arctic Security Cutters, a new class of medium polar icebreakers. The Coast Guard later finalized $2.2 billion in contracts for four hulls built by Bollinger, while Finnish shipbuilder Rauma was awarded $1.1 billion for two hulls to be built in Finland. Earlier reporting put Bollinger's figure at $2.1 billion, so the value differs slightly by source.

## Louisiana's role

The cutters will be built at Bollinger's Houma yard, supported by fabrication capacity across multiple Gulf Coast facilities. The first cutter is scheduled for delivery in 2028, with all six due by 2031. The Coast Guard ultimately plans to acquire up to 11 Arctic Security Cutters.
