Louisiana Economic Development's news listing for August 11 is titled "Coca-Cola UNITED Expands Alexandria Operations," announcing growth at the beverage distributor's Central Louisiana site.

## Limited details

The summary available for this article does not include investment totals, job counts or timelines. Readers should consult the state's release for those numbers.

## Context

Alexandria, in Rapides Parish, is a regional hub for Central Louisiana. The state reported that 2025 was the largest year of investment and job creation in its history, with more than $61 billion in capital investment and over 9,300 direct new jobs, a backdrop for smaller expansions like this one.
