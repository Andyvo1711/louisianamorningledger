Quality Companies announced a two-site manufacturing expansion across Acadiana, according to Louisiana Economic Development and KATC. The project is expected to create 125 direct new jobs at its new Jeanerette facility.

## Timeline

Construction began in July 2026 and the Jeanerette facility is expected to be completed in December 2026, according to the state's project listing. The second site is in the Youngsville area, based on an Area Development listing dated July 20.

## Why it matters

Jeanerette, in Iberia Parish, is a small community where a 125-job manufacturing project is a significant local employer addition. The state's announcement frames the investment as part of broader momentum in Acadiana's manufacturing and energy supply chain.

The reports reviewed do not list the capital investment total or wage levels.
