SLB is more than doubling its footprint at the Shreveport Business Park campus, according to Louisiana Economic Development and a Bossier Press item dated August 24. The campus makes digital infrastructure and data center equipment.

## Jobs and investment

State officials said the expansion could add 744 indirect jobs. One source says construction was slated to begin in January 2026.

## A data center hub in the making

The announcement fits a broader pattern in northwest Louisiana, where Amazon announced a $12 billion plan for data center campuses in Caddo and Bossier parishes in February, and where other firms are expanding to serve the sector.

The reports reviewed do not state the dollar value of SLB's investment.
