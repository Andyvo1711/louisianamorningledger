SpaceX announced on August 25 in Abbeville that it will build a $100 billion launch facility on Pecan Island in Vermilion Parish, which most outlets call "Starbase Louisiana." The facility would occupy roughly 125,000 acres and become the fourth and largest of SpaceX's launch complexes.

## What the company says

Construction is expected to begin in 2027, with a first launch as soon as 2029. SpaceX described a "self-sustaining spaceport" with propellant production, power generation, deep-water shipping capabilities, vehicle processing facilities and an airport. The site is on a former Exxon property, according to The Next Web. Vermilion Parish residents are promised steep discounts on Starlink internet service.

## Concerns

Environmental advocates have raised concerns, noting that Vermilion Parish sits in wetlands far more sensitive than the south Texas coast. Forecasts of jobs and investment are projections rather than commitments.
