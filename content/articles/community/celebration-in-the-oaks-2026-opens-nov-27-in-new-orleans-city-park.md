Celebration in the Oaks, New Orleans City Park's holiday lights display, opens Nov. 27, kicking off the winter holiday lineup, according to a listing for November 2026. The event runs through January 1.

## How to visit

Walking and driving tours are available. The event is held beneath City Park's live oaks, one of the largest stands in the world, according to the park.

## Details

The listing reviewed does not give ticket prices or hours. Guests should check City Park's calendar before planning.
