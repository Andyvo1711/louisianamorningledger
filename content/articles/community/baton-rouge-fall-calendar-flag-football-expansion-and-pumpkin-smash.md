Baton Rouge Parents magazine's November 2026 issue reports that LECJF, BREC, National PAL and NFL FLAG have partnered to expand access to youth flag football across East Baton Rouge Parish.

## Family fun

The Global Wildlife Center in Folsom has a Pumpkin Smash back through the end of November, WBRZ reported, and the Greater Baton Rouge State Fair appears among November events.

## Details

The summaries reviewed do not give registration details or ticket prices.
