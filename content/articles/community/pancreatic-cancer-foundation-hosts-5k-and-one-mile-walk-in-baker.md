A pancreatic cancer foundation hosted a 5K run and one-mile walk in Baker, WAFB reported on November 1. November is Pancreatic Cancer Awareness Month.

## Community fundraising

Charity runs and walks are a common way for families affected by the disease to raise funds for research and patient support. The video item reviewed does not list the foundation's name, the distance fundraising goal or the number of participants.
