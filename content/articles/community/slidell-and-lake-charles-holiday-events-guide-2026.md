A 2026 holiday events guide lists Christmas Under the Stars Dec. 11-12 and 18-19 at Griffith Park in Slidell, and an Olde Towne Slidell Christmas Golf Cart Parade on Dec. 13. Another listing describes lights on Front Street in Olde Towne Slidell from Dec. 1 to Jan. 3.

## Other communities

Lake Charles has a Light Up the Lakefront event at Millennium Park, listed for November 29 at 2 p.m., with extended park hours, light displays and a Christmas walking parade. The Houma Christmas Festival & Parade is also on listings.

## Caution

The Slidell and Lake Charles dates conflict across sources and some listings do not state a year, so confirm times with city and parish calendars.
