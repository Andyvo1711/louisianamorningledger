The Natchitoches Parish Fair returns October 11-17 at the fairgrounds, according to the city's event listings, with rides, live entertainment and exhibits across the week.

## More October fun

Dark Woods Haunted Attraction runs October 2-31, one of several seasonal attractions on the parish calendar.

## A parish calendar

Natchitoches, known for its historic downtown along Cane River Lake, hosts several fall events. Dates come from the city's calendar and should be confirmed with organizers.
