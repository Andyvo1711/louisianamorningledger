A grassroots recall campaign against Gov. Jeff Landry is officially over after failing to collect enough signatures, according to the Louisiana Secretary of State, Axios New Orleans reported on November 9.

## The political backdrop

Landry tied the end of the effort to voters' confidence in the Republican Party after Louisiana narrowly elected Rep. Julia Letlow to a U.S. Senate seat the week before. In a final stretch of that race, Democratic strategist James Carville told the Louisiana Illuminator the contest was competitive, according to an Axios item.

## What comes next

The Lens, republishing Illuminator reporting on November 13, wrote that Democrats are looking toward the 2027 governor's race, citing Landry's sagging approval ratings. A November 19 Illuminator analysis by Greg LaRose argued the Black voter surge was decades in the making, according to Southern advocates.
