Boo at the Zoo at Audubon Zoo in New Orleans is scheduled for October 16-18, 2026, according to fall festival guides. One guide describes it as a daytime celebration for families.

## Other zoo events nearby

The Baton Rouge Zoo, operated by BREC, also hosts its own Boo at the Zoo in October. Audubon's newsroom has previously described the New Orleans event as spooky family fun with costumes, treats and activities.

## Details to check

The listings reviewed do not provide ticket prices or hours, so families should check Audubon's website.
