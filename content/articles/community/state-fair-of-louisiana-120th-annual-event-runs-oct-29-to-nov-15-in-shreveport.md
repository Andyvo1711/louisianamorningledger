The 120th State Fair of Louisiana is scheduled for October 29 through November 15, 2026, at the Louisiana State Fairgrounds in Shreveport. The fair operates for 14 days and is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.

## A long tradition

The Bossier Chamber of Commerce announced that the State Fair of Louisiana would hold its 120th annual event in Shreveport. Local news reported that the fair returns with new rides and food vendors.

## Details

The sources reviewed do not list admission prices or the new rides.
