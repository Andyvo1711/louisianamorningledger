Communities across the Baton Rouge area prepared to honor military veterans on Wednesday, November 11, with ceremonies, parades and memorial tributes, WAFB reported on November 10.

## A day of recognition

Veterans Day events typically include wreath-laying ceremonies, school programs and parades, and many local governments and nonprofits offer free meals or discounts for service members. WAFB's guide lists the schedule of events across the metro area.

## How to find an event

The Louisiana Department of Veterans Affairs and parish governments post local ceremonies. The sources reviewed for this article include only the WAFB guide headline and summary, so readers should consult that guide for times and locations.
