Baton Rouge Metropolitan Airport has partnered with AI company Metropolis for checkout-free parking, the first airport in Louisiana to use the company's drive-in, drive-out system, according to WBRZ.

## More options

In February 2026, the airport also launched premium reserved parking and expanded EV charging, WAFB reported. A business newsletter roundup this fall said the AI-enabled parking technology was rolling out in early December, so timing details differ by source.

## Growth

Business Report has said the airport is coming off back-to-back record passenger years.
