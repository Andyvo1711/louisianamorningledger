Louisiana Tech began its first season in the Sun Belt Conference in 2026 after a settlement with Conference USA cleared the move. The football team hosts Louisiana (Lafayette) at Joe Aillet Stadium in Ruston, with Louisiana Tech's listing saying September 26 and Louisiana's own release saying October 10, so the date should be confirmed.

## Other teams

Louisiana Tech's soccer schedule includes home games against Nicholls on August 13 and McNeese on September 3. The Bulldogs also added Nicholls to their 2027 football schedule, for September 11, 2027 in Ruston.

## Why it matters

The move gives Louisiana's in-state rivalries a conference setting: Louisiana Tech now shares a league with Louisiana, adding a Sun Belt game in Ruston.
