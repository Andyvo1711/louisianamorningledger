Two heritage festivals anchor early October in Natchitoches Parish. The Robeline Heritage Festival runs October 2-3, celebrating the history and culture of one of Louisiana's oldest villages. The St. Augustine Creole Festival is listed for October 10-12 at historic St. Augustine Catholic Church in Isle Brevelle.

## Why they matter

The Creole festival celebrates the rich heritage of the Cane River region, whose Creole communities have deep roots in Louisiana history.

Dates come from the city's event calendar and should be confirmed with organizers.
