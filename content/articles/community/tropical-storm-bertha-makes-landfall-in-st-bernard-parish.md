Tropical Storm Bertha made landfall in St. Bernard Parish, Louisiana, on July 22 with maximum sustained winds of 45 mph, according to public radio reports. The main expected impact was flash flooding.

## Earlier storm, lingering effects

Tropical Storm Arthur, the first named storm of the 2026 Atlantic season, formed off the Texas coast on June 17 and brought flooding and possible tornadoes to southeast Louisiana. FEMA assistance was made available to residents and renters in four parishes, and a rice industry report said flooding was still receding from cropland south of central Louisiana in early July.

## What residents should do

Louisiana's state climatologist had said in May that the 2026 season looked average but not to be shrugged off. The sources reviewed do not include damage totals from Bertha.
