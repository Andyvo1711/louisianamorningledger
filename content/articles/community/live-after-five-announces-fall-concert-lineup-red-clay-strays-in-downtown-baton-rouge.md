Live After Five announced its fall concert lineup in August, WAFB reported. The downtown Baton Rouge concert series draws crowds to the city's center after work on selected evenings.

## A big name in November

WBRZ reported that the Red Clay Strays were coming to downtown Baton Rouge this fall, and a Baton Rouge Parents listing for November 2026 includes the band on November 5.

## Details

The WAFB item reviewed does not list the individual acts, so readers should check the series schedule.
