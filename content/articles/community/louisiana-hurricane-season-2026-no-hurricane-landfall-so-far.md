Louisiana has been touched by several storms this year, but no hurricane has made landfall in the state in 2026, according to reporting reviewed for this article.

## The storms

Tropical Storm Arthur brought flooding and tornadoes to southeast Louisiana in June. Tropical Storm Bertha came ashore in St. Bernard Parish on July 22. Edouard, according to The New York Times as summarized in search coverage, formed near the Louisiana coast, moved inland near the Texas-Louisiana border and did not reach hurricane strength. Hurricane Isaias made landfall in the Florida Panhandle in October; a National Weather Service report said its peripheral impacts reached coastal southeast Louisiana, with zero direct or indirect fatalities.

## Outlook

Forecasters had expected a below-normal season because of El Niño. The sources reviewed do not give a final season tally.
