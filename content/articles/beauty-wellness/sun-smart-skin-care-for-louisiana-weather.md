Louisiana's strong sun makes year-round sun protection a core part of any beauty routine, and the American Academy of Dermatology recommends broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher.

## Daily habits

- **Apply sunscreen** 15 minutes before going out and reapply every two hours, or after sweating or swimming.
- **Use about a shot glass worth** for the full body and a nickel-sized amount for the face.
- **Wear a wide-brimmed hat and sunglasses** during outdoor events and festivals.
- **Seek shade** during peak midday hours.

## Humidity tips

Look for lightweight, non-comedogenic gel or fluid formulas that do not feel heavy in humid weather. Check skin regularly and see a dermatologist about any changing moles.
