Snacks can help bridge long gaps between meals, especially when they combine protein or healthy fat with fiber.

## Easy ideas

- **A small handful of pecans** with an apple or satsuma. Pecans provide healthy fats, and Louisiana's harvest arrives in the fall.
- **Greek yogurt** with berries and a drizzle of honey.
- **Hummus with raw vegetables.**
- **Boiled eggs or cheese with whole-grain crackers.**
- **Roasted chickpeas** for a crunchy option.

## Tips

Portion nuts, since they are calorie-dense, and read labels for added sugar. Listening to hunger cues helps you snack when you need it, not out of habit.
