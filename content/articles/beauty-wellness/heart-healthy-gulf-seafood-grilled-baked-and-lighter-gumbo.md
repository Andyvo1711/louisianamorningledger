Louisiana's seafood tradition offers lean protein and, in some fish, heart-friendly omega-3 fats. Cooking methods matter as much as the catch.

## Make it lighter

- **Grill, bake or boil** instead of deep frying.
- **Use herbs, lemon and Creole seasoning in moderation**; many blends are high in sodium, so taste before adding more.
- **Fill gumbo with vegetables** such as okra, onions, celery and peppers, and use a lighter roux.
- **Choose brown rice or a smaller scoop of white rice.**

## How often

Dietary guidelines suggest eating fish or seafood at least twice a week, favoring varieties lower in mercury. Pregnant women should follow FDA and state guidance on which fish to avoid.
