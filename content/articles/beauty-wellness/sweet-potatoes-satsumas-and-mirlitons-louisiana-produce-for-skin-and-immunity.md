Fall brings some of Louisiana's best produce to markets, and it happens to be rich in nutrients linked to healthy skin and immune function.

## What to look for

- **Sweet potatoes** are high in beta-carotene and fiber. Bake or roast them rather than candying them to keep added sugar in check.
- **Satsumas** are a sweet, easy-to-peel citrus rich in vitamin C, available in late fall.
- **Mirlitons** (chayote squash) are low in calories and can be stuffed, roasted or added to stews.
- **Greens** such as mustard and collards offer vitamins A, C and K.

## Tips

Eat the rainbow, buy in season and store sweet potatoes in a cool, dark place. Local farmers markets are a good way to find fresh produce.
