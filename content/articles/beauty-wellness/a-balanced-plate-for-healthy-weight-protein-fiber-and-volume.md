Healthy weight management is about sustainable habits, not extreme diets. If you have medical conditions or a history of disordered eating, work with a clinician.

## Build satisfying meals

- **Protein at each meal** from fish, poultry, eggs, beans or Greek yogurt supports fullness and muscle maintenance.
- **Fiber-rich plants** such as vegetables, beans and whole grains slow digestion.
- **Healthy fats in modest amounts**, such as olive oil and nuts.
- **Mindful portions** of favorite foods, so traditional dishes can remain part of the week.

## Louisiana twist

Grilled or boiled shrimp and crawfish are lean proteins. Boiled crawfish with corn and potatoes can be a balanced meal when portions of buttery sides stay moderate. Choose a lighter roux or add extra vegetables to gumbo.

## Small changes

Drink water before reaching for a snack, walk after meals, and aim for steady progress.
