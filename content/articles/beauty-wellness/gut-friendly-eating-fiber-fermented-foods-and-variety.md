Research on the gut microbiome is evolving, but many nutrition experts agree that variety and fiber are good starting points.

## Simple steps

- **Eat many kinds of plants** each week, including beans, whole grains, fruits, vegetables, nuts and seeds.
- **Add fermented foods** such as yogurt with live cultures, kefir, kimchi or sauerkraut.
- **Increase fiber gradually** and drink water to avoid bloating.
- **Limit ultra-processed foods** that are high in sugar and refined starches.

## When to seek help

Ongoing abdominal pain, unexplained weight loss, blood in stool or major changes in bowel habits warrant a medical evaluation. Probiotic supplements are not necessary for most healthy people.
