The Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity per week for adults, plus muscle-strengthening activities on two or more days.

## A sample week

- **Monday, Wednesday, Friday:** 30-minute brisk walk.
- **Tuesday and Saturday:** 20-30 minutes of bodyweight strength moves such as squats, wall push-ups and glute bridges.
- **Thursday or Sunday:** gentle stretching or yoga.

## Staying consistent

Start where you are, add time gradually and choose activities you enjoy. In Louisiana, early mornings and evenings are cooler for outdoor walks in summer, and cooler fall weather is ideal for parks and bayou trails. People with health conditions should check with their clinician before starting a new routine.
