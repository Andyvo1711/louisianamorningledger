Healthy nails and soft hands depend on daily habits as much as on salon appointments.

## Everyday care

- **Moisturize hands and cuticles** with hand cream or cuticle oil, especially after washing.
- **File in one direction** with a fine-grit file rather than tearing or biting nails.
- **Wear gloves** when washing dishes or cleaning.
- **Do not cut cuticles at home**; they protect the nail from infection.
- **Take breaks from gel or acrylic products** if nails become thin or brittle.

## At the salon

Choose licensed salons that sterilize tools. Speak up if something feels painful, and ask about ingredients if you have allergies. See a clinician for persistent discoloration, pain or swelling.
