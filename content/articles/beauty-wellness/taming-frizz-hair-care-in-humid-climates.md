Humidity makes hair absorb moisture from the air, which can lead to frizz, especially for curly or coarse hair.

## Tips

- **Condition regularly** with a leave-in conditioner or light oil to seal the cuticle.
- **Detangle gently** with a wide-tooth comb on damp hair.
- **Limit heat styling** and use a heat protectant when you do.
- **Rinse with cool water** after conditioning to help smooth the cuticle.
- **Sleep on silk or satin** to reduce friction.

## Salon advice

A stylist can recommend a cut and treatment suited to your hair type, and protective styles can help during peak humidity months.
