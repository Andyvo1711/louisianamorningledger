Louisiana's heat and humidity can lead to heavy sweating, especially outdoors. There is no single right amount of water for everyone, but a few practical guidelines help.

## Practical cues

- Drink to thirst, and drink more if you are active or outdoors.
- Pale yellow urine generally suggests good hydration; dark urine suggests you need more fluids.
- Foods count: fruits and vegetables such as watermelon, cucumbers and oranges supply water.
- For long, intense activity in the heat, electrolytes may help.

## Beyond skin deep

Mild dehydration can contribute to headaches, fatigue and dry skin. Older adults and people on certain medications should ask their clinicians about fluid needs.

## Smart habits

Carry a refillable bottle, drink a glass with each meal and limit sugary beverages.
