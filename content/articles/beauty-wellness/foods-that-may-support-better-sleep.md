Good sleep depends on many things, but what and when you eat can play a role. None of these foods is a cure for insomnia, and anyone with persistent sleep trouble should talk with a clinician.

## Foods worth trying

- **Kiwi.** Small studies have linked eating kiwi in the evening with modest improvements in sleep quality, though more research is needed.
- **Tart cherries.** Tart cherry juice contains small amounts of melatonin and has been studied for sleep, with mixed results.
- **Oats and whole grains.** A small bowl of oatmeal provides complex carbohydrates that many people find settling.
- **Nuts such as pecans and almonds.** They provide magnesium and healthy fats; Louisiana pecans make an easy local choice.
- **Warm, caffeine-free drinks.** Chamomile tea or warm milk is as much about routine as chemistry.

## Habits that matter more

Try to finish large meals two to three hours before bed, limit caffeine after midday and keep alcohol modest, since it can fragment sleep.
