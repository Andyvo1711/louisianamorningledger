Stress and poor sleep often feed each other. A consistent evening routine can signal to your body that it is time to rest.

## Try this

- **Set a regular bedtime** and wake time, even on weekends.
- **Dim lights and silence devices** 30 to 60 minutes before bed.
- **Do a short wind-down ritual**, such as stretching, a warm shower, journaling or slow breathing.
- **Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet.**

## Skin and sleep

Evening is also a good time for a simple skin routine: cleanse, moisturize and, in dry months, add a gentle night cream.

If stress or sleep problems persist, talk with a health professional.
