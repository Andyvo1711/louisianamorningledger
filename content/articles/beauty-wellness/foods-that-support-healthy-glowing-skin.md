No food will erase wrinkles, but a balanced diet supplies the nutrients skin needs to repair itself and defend against daily stress.

## Nutrients to look for

- **Vitamin C** supports collagen production. Louisiana citrus such as satsumas, along with strawberries and bell peppers, are good sources.
- **Beta-carotene** from sweet potatoes, carrots and leafy greens is converted to vitamin A, which supports skin renewal.
- **Vitamin E** from nuts and seeds helps protect cell membranes.
- **Omega-3 fats** from fatty fish, walnuts and flaxseed may help keep skin supple.
- **Water** matters too, though drinking more than you need does not guarantee glowing skin.

## What to limit

Diets high in added sugar and highly processed foods are associated with poorer skin health in some studies, and nothing replaces sunscreen.
