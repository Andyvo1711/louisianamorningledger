Chronic inflammation is linked with many health conditions, and diet is one factor that may influence it. There is no magic anti-inflammatory food, but patterns matter.

## What to emphasize

- **Vegetables and fruit** in many colors, including berries, leafy greens and tomatoes.
- **Fish** such as salmon, sardines and Gulf catches.
- **Olive oil, nuts and seeds.**
- **Whole grains and beans.**
- **Herbs and spices** such as turmeric and ginger, in food.

## What to limit

Sugary drinks, refined grains, processed meats and heavy frying are associated with higher inflammation markers. Pair eating habits with sleep, activity and stress management.
