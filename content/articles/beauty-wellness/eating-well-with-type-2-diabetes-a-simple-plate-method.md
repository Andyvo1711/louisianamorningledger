For many people managing type 2 diabetes, the plate method is a simple, flexible way to build meals without counting every gram. A registered dietitian or diabetes care team can tailor advice to individual needs and medications.

## The basic plate

- **Half the plate: non-starchy vegetables** such as okra, green beans, greens, cabbage, peppers and squash.
- **A quarter: lean protein** such as grilled fish, shrimp, chicken or beans.
- **A quarter: high-fiber carbohydrates** such as brown rice, sweet potato, beans or whole-grain bread.

## Louisiana-friendly tips

Okra and mirliton add bulk and fiber to gumbo and stews. Consider smaller portions of rice, extra vegetables and lean proteins, and go easy on sweet drinks.

## Other basics

Fiber slows the absorption of glucose, and spreading carbohydrates through the day can help avoid large spikes. Sugary drinks tend to raise blood sugar quickly, so water, unsweetened tea or sparkling water are better everyday picks.
