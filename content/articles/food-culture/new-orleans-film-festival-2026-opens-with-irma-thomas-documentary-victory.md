The 37th New Orleans Film Festival was set for October, opening with the documentary "Victory," about Irma Thomas, according to the New Orleans Film Society. Sources give slightly different dates, with one listing October 21-27 and another October 22. The festival's own page lists next year's festival for October 21-26, 2027.

## Organization news

In January the New Orleans Film Society named Dodd Loomis executive director and Doug Spearman board president. Loyola University's filmmaking program has also had a strong showing at past festivals.

## Detail

The sources reviewed do not provide a full list of competition films or attendance.
