Beignet Fest 2026 was set for Saturday, November 7 at the New Orleans City Park Festival Grounds from about 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. One listing calls it the ninth annual edition.

## What was on offer

Tickets included general admission and VIP options, with children 10 and under free and prices of $20 to $50 on Time Out. Reported performers included Imagination Movers, Amanda Shaw and the Cute Guys and Big Sam's Funky Nation, with Maggie Koerner and Dragon Smoke named in May.

## Recognition and purpose

Beignet Fest earned the No. 8 spot in the 2026 USA TODAY 10Best Readers' Choice Awards for specialty food festivals. Proceeds benefit the Beignet Fest Foundation, a 501(c)(3) supporting programs for autistic youth and their families in Greater New Orleans.
