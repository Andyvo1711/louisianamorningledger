Bayou Bacchanal, listed for November 7, brings Caribbean cuisine alongside music and dance, according to a neworleans.com listing. It was one of several food-focused events that weekend, together with Beignet Fest on November 7 and the Oak Street Po-Boy Festival on November 8.

## A crowded calendar

The weekend also included the HNOC Food Forum on gumbo. LUNA Fête, a light-art festival, followed the next weekend, with sources saying November 13-15.

## What the listing doesn't say

The listing reviewed does not provide Bayou Bacchanal's venue or ticket price.
