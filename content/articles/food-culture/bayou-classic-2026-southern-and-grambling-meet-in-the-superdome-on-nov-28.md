The 53rd Bayou Classic is set for Saturday, November 28 at 1 p.m. at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans, with the weekend running November 26-28. Southern hosts Grambling State in its regular-season finale.

## Series and traditions

Southern won the 2025 game 28-27, putting the Jaguars ahead in the all-time series 28-24. The Battle of the Bands & Greek Show is part of the weekend, and football and Battle of the Bands tickets went on sale May 8.

## Planning

An official hotel block was offered at hotels within walking distance or a short ride from the Superdome. The game had not been played at the time of writing.
