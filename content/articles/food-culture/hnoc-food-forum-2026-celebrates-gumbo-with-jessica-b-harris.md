The Historic New Orleans Collection's 2026 Food Forum focused on gumbo, Louisiana's official state dish. The forum was listed for Saturday, November 7 at HNOC's Williams Research Center, 410 Chartres Street, from 9:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.

## Speakers and events

Food historian Dr. Jessica B. Harris headlined, examining the origins, variations and deep community connections of gumbo. The forum cost $50, a separate Food Fête tasting at 520 Royal Street cost $35, and an optional $45 lunch and cooking demonstration with Chef Dianne "Gumbo Marie" Honoré Montana was offered at the New Orleans School of Cooking.

## Sponsor

Chef Paul Prudhomme's Magic Seasoning Blends presented the event.

No post-event coverage was found in the sources reviewed.
