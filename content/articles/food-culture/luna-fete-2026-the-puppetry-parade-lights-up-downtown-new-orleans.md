LUNA Fête returns to downtown New Orleans this weekend, Axios reported on November 12, with the festival beginning Friday and running through Sunday. A different listing gave November 14-17, so that outlier should be treated with caution.

## Theme and footprint

The 2026 theme is "The Puppetry Parade," with larger-than-life, handmade puppets plus the festival's signature projection mapping and light sculptures. It starts at Gallier Hall and Lafayette Square and ends at the Piazza d'Italia.

## Background

Arts New Orleans moved the free festival from November 2025 to November 2026 to ensure it returns at full creative and community impact.
