Southern Decadence, one of New Orleans's largest annual events, ran Thursday, September 3 through Monday, September 7, 2026, with the theme "Phoenix Rising: Together We Rise." The Grand Marshal parade was Sunday, September 6.

## Scale and traditions

One guide calls it the fifth-largest annual event in New Orleans, and 2026 was the 54th edition. A drag brunch at House of Blues was held September 6.

## Tourism angle

The festival arrives during the late-summer lull and draws visitors to hotels and restaurants in the French Quarter and Marigny. The sources reviewed include listings and guides but no post-event attendance figures.
