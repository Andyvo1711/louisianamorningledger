A listing for the Tremé Fall Fest says it returned on October 25, with food, live music, art and performances. The neighborhood is often described as the oldest Black neighborhood in the United States and is home to Congo Square and Louis Armstrong Park.

## Another gumbo festival, paused

A separate calendar listed the Tremé Creole Gumbo Festival for November 14-15 at Louis Armstrong Park, but another events calendar said it was paused for 2026, so readers should check with organizers.

## A caution

The Tremé Fall Fest information comes from an event listing and a roundup rather than a news report.
