Satchmo SummerFest took place August 1-2 at the New Orleans Jazz Museum, a free weekend honoring Louis Armstrong. French Quarter Festivals, Inc. produces the event, and sources count it as its 25th or 26th year.

## The music

The lineup spanned traditional jazz, brass and marching bands, funk, R&B and jazz fusion. A wrap-up headline described the festival as ending on a high note, but the full text was not available for this article.

## Food and culture

Past editions have paired music with Creole and New Orleans cuisine and educational programming at the museum, which is housed in the Old U.S. Mint.
