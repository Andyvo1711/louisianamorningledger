The 2026 Essence Festival of Culture ran July 3-5 in New Orleans, with signature programming at the Caesars Superdome and daytime events at the Ernest N. Morial Convention Center from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central.

## The lineup

Cardi B, George Clinton and Patti LaBelle headlined, with evening concerts including Babyface, Leon Thomas, Public Enemy and George Clinton & Parliament-Funkadelic with Friends. Cardi B and Latto made their festival debuts on the Superdome stage, and Brandy and Monica were also announced.

## Behind the scenes

Coverage noted the organizers faced financial disputes: reports said the festival still owed the convention center more than $400,000 from last year's event. The sources reviewed did not report attendance figures.

Single-night tickets went on sale May 13.
