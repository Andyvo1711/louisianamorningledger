The Oak Street Po-Boy Festival took place Sunday, November 8 between the 8100 and 8700 blocks of Oak Street Uptown, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., according to its organizers. The festival page calls it the 18th annual.

## Creative takes

WWNO's "Where Y'eat" report from November 5 said some 2026 vendors served a sushi roll crossed with a po-boy from Seither's Seafood and Mexican birria and carnitas po-boys from Aguasanta.

## A sandwich with a story

The po-boy is said to have originated during a streetcar strike in the late 1920s, when it was made to support striking workers.

The sources reviewed did not include a 2026 music lineup.
