WLOX's September 27 fall festival guide lists events across metro New Orleans. Abita Fall Fest on October 2 at the Abita Springs Trailhead Park offers two stages of live music, art and food trucks. The Fall Garden Festival on October 3 at the New Orleans Botanical Garden features more than 50 plant vendors, educational exhibits and live music.

## More on the calendar

LUNA Fête, a free downtown light-art festival, returns in November with the theme "The Puppetry Parade." Other items on neworleans.com's roundup include Crescent City Blues & BBQ Fest, Gentilly Fest, Tremé Fall Fest and National Fried Chicken Fest.
