Capital Area United Way's Jambalaya Jam took place Thursday, October 15 from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. at North Boulevard Town Square in downtown Baton Rouge. Regions Bank presented the 2026 event.

## The competition

Cooking teams compete for the jambalaya title, with live music and family activities. In 2025, more than 65 cooking teams competed, raising over $174,000 for CAUW initiatives across its 10-parish service area.

## Past pricing

Past tickets ranged from about $10 for to-go plates to higher-priced VIP options. The sources reviewed do not list 2026 prices.
