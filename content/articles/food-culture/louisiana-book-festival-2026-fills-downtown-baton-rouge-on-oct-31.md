The 22nd Louisiana Book Festival was set for Saturday, October 31 from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the State Library of Louisiana in downtown Baton Rouge. Admission was free.

## Honoree and reading program

Farrah Rochon was named the 27th Louisiana Writer Award recipient, with the award presented during the festival ceremony. The One Book, One Festival discussion centered on Anne Rice's "Interview with the Vampire." Writing workshops (WordShops) were held the day before, October 30.

## Scale

Past coverage says the festival attracts roughly 20,000 people each year. The author roster was not available in the sources reviewed.
