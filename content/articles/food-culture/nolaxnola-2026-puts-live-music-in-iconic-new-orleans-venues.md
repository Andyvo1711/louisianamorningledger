NOLAxNOLA 2026 runs September 24 through October 4, focused on live music at iconic venues, according to the city tourism organization's event page.

## A music-first fall

The series opens the fall festival season in a city where live music is a core part of the visitor economy. It is listed alongside Abita Fall Fest on October 2, the Fall Garden Festival at the New Orleans Botanical Garden on October 3, and a Tremé Fall Fest listed for October 25.

## What the listing doesn't say

The event page reviewed does not detail the venues or lineups, so visitors should consult it before planning.
