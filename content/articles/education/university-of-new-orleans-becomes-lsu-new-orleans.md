The University of New Orleans began operating as LSU New Orleans (LSUNO) on July 1, 2026, as the Lakefront campus formally returned to the LSU System, according to Axios New Orleans.

## A new name and a new system

The change follows a transition announced in late 2025 that moves the campus under LSU System governance. In June, the Board of Regents' meetings included updates on the UNO transition, and the LSU Board of Supervisors' September 4 agenda listed a proposed mission statement for LSU New Orleans among the items before its academic committee. Those agenda items show the board's work on the new institution, though they are not a record of what was ultimately approved.

## Why it matters locally

The Lakefront campus is a major source of degrees in engineering, business and the maritime fields for the New Orleans region. Moving it into the LSU System puts it alongside the state's flagship in a more unified structure, one of several consolidations LSU leaders have pursued this year.

Students and staff should expect the new LSUNO name to appear gradually on signage, web addresses and official documents as the transition continues.
