The Louisiana Department of Education released 2026 LEAP results on July 6, reporting that 36% of students in grades 3-12 scored Mastery or Advanced, a two-percentage-point increase and the highest statewide performance on record for those grades.

## Where scores moved

According to the department, students improved or held steady in 28 of the 31 LEAP-tested grades and courses. At the high school level, the share reaching Mastery or above rose in Civics (up 5 points), English II (up 3), U.S. History (up 3) and Algebra I (up 2). Social studies in grades 3-8 improved by 2 points. School systems also moved forward: 75% improved from 2025 to 2026, 10% held steady and 15% declined.

State Superintendent Cade Brumley said the state cannot be satisfied and must build on the gains as expectations rise.

## What comes next

The results carry extra weight this year. They are the first LEAP scores to feed into School Performance Scores under Louisiana's revised accountability system, branded Grow. Achieve. Thrive., in which Mastery is the benchmark for proficiency. The state has cautioned that some school and system ratings may look different under the new formula, and the first official scores under the revised system are expected later in 2026.

The record also comes with a caveat: roughly 64% of students still fall short of Mastery on LEAP, so most students remain below the state's proficiency bar.
