McNeese State University in Lake Charles is entering a new chapter in leadership. A Louisiana Board of Regents meeting document says State Sen. Mike Reese will become the ninth president of McNeese State University in July.

## Background

Earlier in the year, the University of Louisiana System Board of Supervisors appointed interim presidents for both UL Lafayette and McNeese State. Reese's selection ends the interim period at McNeese, which serves Southwest Louisiana, including a region still rebuilding after the hurricanes of 2020.

## What students and employers should watch

McNeese anchors workforce training for the area's energy, industrial and health-care employers. The Regents document reviewed for this story confirms the planned July start but does not detail Reese's priorities. Those will likely become clearer in his first campus remarks and in the system's budget requests for the coming year.

Meanwhile, McNeese's football program opens its 2026 season against Stephen F. Austin and Texas Wesleyan, according to the university's schedule release, with a game against LSU also on the calendar.
