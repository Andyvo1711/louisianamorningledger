Tulane University welcomed the Class of 2030 to campus in mid-August, with President Michael Fitts addressing new students at Convocation in remarks that Tulane's news office summarized as combining "World Cup spirit" with "Lasso lessons."

## Orientation and the first day

Tulane also published a story on the Hullabaloo Hello orientation program, which helps incoming students find their place on campus. The university's academic calendar lists August 17, 2026 as the first day of classes for the full fall term and the first eight-week term.

Tulane's news office also reported that new faculty members were joining the university for the fall semester, and that ten Tulanians were recognized by the National Science Foundation.

## An Uptown tradition

Gibson Hall, the Romanesque landmark at the front of the Uptown campus on St. Charles Avenue, remains the visual anchor for many opening-week events. The university did not publish enrollment figures for the incoming class in the items reviewed for this story.
