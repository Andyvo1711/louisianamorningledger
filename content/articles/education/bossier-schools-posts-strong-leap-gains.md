Bossier Parish Schools posted strong gains on the 2026 LEAP, according to an item distributed by the Bossier Chamber of Commerce, as Louisiana reached its highest proficiency rate on record for grades 3-12.

## The statewide backdrop

The Louisiana Department of Education reported on July 6 that 36% of students reached Mastery or above, with improvement in 28 of 31 tested grades and courses. Seventy-five percent of school systems improved.

## What the Chamber's note signals

Local business groups increasingly track school performance because workforce readiness is a main driver of regional growth. The chamber's headline describes the Bossier system's gains as strong, but the summary reviewed for this article does not include specific percentages.

Bossier is part of the fast-growing Shreveport-Bossier region in northwest Louisiana, where large employers and data-center investments have raised the stakes for workforce preparation.
