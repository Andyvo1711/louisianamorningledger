East Baton Rouge Parish Schools announced on July 6 that its 2026 LEAP results represent its strongest post-pandemic performance, under Superintendent Lamont Cole. WBRZ's coverage the same week framed the district's results as record results in the 2025-26 school year.

## Statewide setting

The state reported that 36% of students in grades 3-12 reached Mastery or above, with 75% of school systems improving.

## Questions parents may ask

District-level releases typically list grade-by-grade and subject-by-subject changes. The coverage reviewed for this article does not break out EBR's individual numbers, so families seeking details should check the district's release and the state's school-system report cards. Student-level reports have been going home through school systems, with state-produced parent guides to help families read them.
