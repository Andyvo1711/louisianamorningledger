As the 2026-27 school year approaches, Louisiana school systems are working through a state mandate to raise pay for teachers and support staff after the legislative session ended without a permanent funding deal.

## What the order does

Gov. Jeff Landry signed an executive order on June 2, 2026 (Executive Order 26-047) that reduced the Minimum Foundation Program by $168 million and redirected those funds through the Department of Education. Under the order, teachers are set to receive at least $2,000 and support staff at least $1,000. A statewide ballot measure on May 16, Amendment 3, would have allowed up to a $2,250 raise for certified personnel and up to $1,125 for uncertified staff; the sources reviewed do not report how voters decided it.

## Pressure on local budgets

The Louisiana Department of Education issued guidance saying step increases tied to years of service or added education do not count toward the required raise, which has prompted districts to examine whether their budgets can absorb the mandate. WBRZ reported that some districts expect tough budget choices.

A legislative task force has been created to look for a permanent funding solution, and Landry has said the order is intended to make 2026-27 the last year teachers have to question whether a raise will return.
