The Louisiana Department of Education announced on July 3 that school systems would begin sharing 2026 state assessment student reports with families, accompanied by parent guides intended to make the results easier to understand.

## Why the guides exist

Under the state's revised accountability approach, Mastery is the benchmark for proficiency. A student report can be hard to interpret without context, so the department produced guides explaining the achievement levels and what they mean for a child's next steps.

## What parents can do

Families who receive a report can ask their child's teacher how the results compare with classroom performance and what supports are available. The Department's release, titled "School systems will begin sharing 2026 state assessment student reports with families," points parents to the guides it published alongside.

Statewide results, announced days later, showed 36% of students in grades 3-12 at Mastery or above.
