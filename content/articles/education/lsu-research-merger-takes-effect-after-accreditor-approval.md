LSU announced on August 5 that the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC) approved the merger of LSU Health Sciences Center New Orleans with a related LSU entity at its June 2026 meeting. LSU's own notice states that the merger became effective on August 1, formally recognizing the newly organized institution as Louisiana's flagship university and a unified research enterprise.

## The plan behind the merger

The LSU Board of Supervisors approved the consolidation plan on December 11, 2025. It brings together the research-intensive campuses of LSU Health Sciences Center in Shreveport and New Orleans, Pennington Biomedical Research Center and the LSU AgCenter under the LSU flagship A&M institution.

## Leadership and academics

LSU also said Joseph P. Messina would begin as provost on August 3, pending approval by the Board of Supervisors, and that the university would begin offering Louisiana's first bachelor's degree in artificial intelligence this fall in Baton Rouge.

Reporting reviewed for this story does not spell out how the merger affects the names or leadership structure of individual health campuses, which LSU may clarify as the integration proceeds.
