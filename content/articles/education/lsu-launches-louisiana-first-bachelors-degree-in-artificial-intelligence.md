LSU is offering Louisiana's first bachelor's degree in artificial intelligence this fall at its Baton Rouge campus, a program the university announced on March 27 after approval from the Louisiana Board of Regents.

## What students will study

According to LSU, the Bachelor of Science in AI required the creation of 20 new courses and includes internships, undergraduate research and a senior capstone. The university said some classes could later be offered online or in a hybrid format.

## Context

The program is housed in LSU Engineering. A December 2025 LSU article had said the degree might start as early as spring 2027 if approved, but LSU's March announcement and its program page list fall 2026 as the start. The university has described the degree as part of its effort to prepare Louisiana for digital transformation in the workforce.

The launch arrives as the Board of Regents has also approved the state's first accelerated bachelor's degrees, a separate change that could shorten the time students need to finish certain programs.
