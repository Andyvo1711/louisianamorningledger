The Southern University System Foundation held its annual SU Give Day in late August with a goal of raising $25 million for its campuses. WBRZ described the effort on August 27, and WAFB profiled how the campaign helps keep students' college plans alive.

## The campaign

The goal is part of "Are You In?," a broader campaign to raise more than $25 million by the end of 2026. Funds support student scholarships, faculty research and program grants, and campus operations and capital improvements.

## The numbers so far

Sources disagree on whether Give Day fell on August 27 or 28. The campaign's GiveCampus page showed $13,567,823 donated, or 54% of the $25 million goal, from 4,241 donors. That figure covers the full campaign, not Give Day alone, and no separate Give Day total was found.

Alumni and supporters can still give through the campaign page, which also describes the 1880 Society, the foundation's recognition group for donors.
