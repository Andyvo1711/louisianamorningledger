Southern University in Baton Rouge welcomed new and returning students for the 2026-27 school year in mid-August. WAFB covered freshmen moving into residence halls on August 10, and WBRZ reported that students were on campus for the start of the fall semester.

## Key dates

The university set a three-day move-in schedule for August 10-13, and classes began on Monday, August 17. Southern Athletics also held its Fan Fest on August 15, which the department billed as welcoming Jaguar Nation back to "The Bluff."

## Growth context

The Southern University System reported a 4% enrollment increase in fall 2025, including a 15% jump in first-time freshmen, according to preliminary figures. The sources reviewed for this story do not include fall 2026 enrollment totals.

The semester ends with the Bayou Classic, when Southern hosts rival Grambling State on November 28 at the Caesars Superdome in New Orleans.
