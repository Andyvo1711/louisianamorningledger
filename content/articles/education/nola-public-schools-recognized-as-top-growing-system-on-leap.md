New Orleans was highlighted in the statewide 2026 LEAP results. WWNO reported on July 8 that 2026 LEAP scores show growth, especially in New Orleans, and NOLA Public Schools said in a press release that it was recognized as a top-growing school system on the 2025-26 results.

## Part of a statewide gain

The statewide numbers released July 6 by the Louisiana Department of Education showed 36% of students in grades 3-12 reaching Mastery or above, the highest on record. The department said 75% of school systems improved.

## The detail still to come

The reporting reviewed for this article does not list the specific point gains in New Orleans, so readers should consult the district's release and the state's data tables for grade-level and subject-level figures. Under the revised accountability system, the first official School Performance Scores reflecting these results are expected later in 2026.
