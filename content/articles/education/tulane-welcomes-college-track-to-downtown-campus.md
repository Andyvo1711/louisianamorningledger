Tulane University announced on August 13 that it is expanding its long-standing collaboration with College Track, the nationally recognized college-success organization, by welcoming it to a newly renovated 10,000-square-foot facility on Tulane's downtown New Orleans campus.

## A deeper partnership

College Track supports students from under-resourced communities from high school through college graduation. Tulane's announcement describes the new space as an expansion of an existing relationship rather than a first-time partnership.

The downtown campus, home to Tulane's health sciences programs, sits close to New Orleans's medical district. Tulane has not been reported in the material reviewed for this article to have released program enrollment numbers or an opening date for services in the new space.

For students and families, the practical takeaway is a physical College Track presence within walking distance of Tulane's downtown classrooms and labs, which the university says deepens the organizations' work together.
