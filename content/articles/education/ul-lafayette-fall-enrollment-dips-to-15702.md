The University of Louisiana at Lafayette is reporting a 2.5% decline in fall enrollment, according to the Louisiana Illuminator. The university counted 15,702 students this fall, down 398 from 16,100 in fall 2025.

## A snapshot in a year of change

The dip comes during a period of leadership turnover across the University of Louisiana System. Earlier this year, the system's board appointed interim leaders for UL Lafayette and McNeese State University.

Enrollment figures for other Louisiana campuses have been mixed: the Southern University System reported a 4% increase in fall 2025, including a 15% increase in first-time freshmen, though the sources reviewed here do not include fall 2026 totals for Southern or Grambling State.

## What to watch

Enrollment drives tuition revenue and state formula funding, so year-over-year shifts of this size are closely watched. The report reviewed for this story does not explain the reasons for the decline, and the university may release a more detailed breakdown by student level and residency later in the semester.
