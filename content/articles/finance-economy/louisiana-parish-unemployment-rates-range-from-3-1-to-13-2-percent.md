Unemployment rates across Louisiana's parishes ranged from 3.1% to 13.2% in May 2026, according to USAFacts. East Carroll Parish had the highest rate, and West Feliciana Parish had the lowest.

## Statewide picture

USAFacts reported that the state's unemployment rate was 4.4% in June. FRED showed total nonfarm employment of about 2,009.2 thousand jobs in June 2026, and a broader U-4 measure of 4.7% for the second quarter.

## Why the spread matters

The gap between rural northeast Louisiana parishes and the Baton Rouge suburbs illustrates why statewide averages mask local conditions. Statewide announcements of major projects, from SpaceX in Vermilion Parish to Meta in Richland Parish, will test whether investment reaches high-unemployment parishes.

The sources reviewed do not specify what drives the gap in East Carroll.
