Beginning July 1, 2026, Louisiana C-corporations can no longer claim the credit for local inventory taxes, a change from a 2025 law (SB 65) that also prohibits estates and trusts from claiming it. Cooperatives and S-corporations can still claim the credit under certain conditions.

## Related tax changes

A 2025 legislative summary described sales tax changes, including making a .45% levy permanent and raising another levy from .97% to 1%, with total state sales and use tax reaching 4.75% beginning January 1, 2030. The corporate franchise tax was repealed for tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2026.

## Who is affected

Businesses with significant inventory, such as retailers and distributors, should check with tax advisers about how the loss of the credit affects their liability. The sources reviewed describe the legal changes but not their estimated fiscal impact.
