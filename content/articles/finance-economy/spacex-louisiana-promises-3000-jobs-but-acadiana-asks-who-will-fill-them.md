The Louisiana Illuminator asked on September 29 whether Acadiana is ready to fill the jobs SpaceX is bringing, noting that what exactly those jobs will be remains unclear.

## What the state projects

Louisiana Economic Development projects 3,000 direct new jobs over 10 years at an average annual salary of $92,600 for SpaceX's planned $100 billion complex in Vermilion Parish. Forecasts like these are projections, not commitments, one commentator noted.

## Incentives

The state offered SpaceX an incentives package conditioned on capital investment, job creation and a $25 million charitable donation to the Community Foundation of Acadiana.

## Local effects

Coastal Machine & Supply announced a $25.5 million Lafayette Parish expansion on September 3, tied to Acadiana's growing space economy. Environmental advocates have raised concerns about building in sensitive wetlands.
