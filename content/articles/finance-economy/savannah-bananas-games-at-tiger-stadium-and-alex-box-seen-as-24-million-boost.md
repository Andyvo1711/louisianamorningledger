LSU Athletics, the Baton Rouge Area Foundation, the Greater Baton Rouge Economic Partnership and Visit Baton Rouge announced on October 15 that the Savannah Bananas will return to Baton Rouge for games in March 2027.

## The schedule

The Bananas play Alex Box Stadium on March 4, 2027, and make their first Tiger Stadium appearance on March 6 as part of the Death Valley Live Stadium Series, against the Firefighters.

## Economic estimates

Visit Baton Rouge estimates economic impact at $24 million for both events, with $1.8 million in taxes collected. The local council approved a similar plan at its October 14 meeting covering tax rebates to promoters and artists.

## Tickets

Suite and club holders will receive email details. For previous games the Bananas used a ticket lottery for general admission, but 2027 processes may differ.
