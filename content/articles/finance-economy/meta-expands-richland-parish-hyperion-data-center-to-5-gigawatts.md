Meta announced on July 13 that it will expand its Hyperion AI data center in Richland Parish from an initial 2 gigawatts to 5 gigawatts of compute capacity, pushing its planned investment in the region beyond $50 billion. Another outlet put the project's total cost above $200 billion, a figure not corroborated by others.

## Local economic effects

Meta said local Louisiana businesses have received more than $1.6 billion in contracts since construction began, and that teacher bonuses in Richland Parish rose from $10,000 last year to more than $50,000 this year, tied to data center tax revenue. Meta also donated $5 million to Louisiana Delta Community College for training scholarships. Fortune reported that Meta quietly bought about 1,400 acres adjacent to its 2,250-acre site.

## Power and policy

Entergy plans to build ten new power plants connected to the project: six in Richland Parish, three in Pointe Coupee Parish and one in St. Charles Parish. Meta says it will cover construction costs rather than Entergy ratepayers. Reporting notes that neither the state nor Richland Parish has released an estimate of how much the tax exemptions will cost over the life of the deal.
