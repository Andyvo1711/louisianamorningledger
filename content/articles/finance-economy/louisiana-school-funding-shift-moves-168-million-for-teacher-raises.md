Gov. Jeff Landry's June 2 executive order reduced the Minimum Foundation Program, the state's school funding formula, by $168 million and redirected those funds through the Department of Education to support teacher and staff raises in 2026-27.

## How the money moves

Teachers are set to receive at least $2,000 and support staff at least $1,000. Districts have been asked to find the money in their budgets, and the Louisiana Department of Education said step increases do not count toward the required raise. Local officials told WBRZ the mandate could mean tough budget choices.

## The longer-term question

Landry also directed education agencies to find non-instructional funds to reallocate. A legislative task force is looking for a permanent funding solution after the session ended without a deal.

The sources reviewed do not report how much each district will receive.
