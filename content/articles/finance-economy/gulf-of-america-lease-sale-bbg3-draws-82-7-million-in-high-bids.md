The federal government's third offshore oil and gas lease sale under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, called BBG3, drew $82,689,756 in high bids for 59 blocks in the Gulf of America, with the sale held in New Orleans on August 12.

## The numbers

Sixteen companies submitted 69 bids totaling $99,476,285, according to the Bureau of Ocean Energy Management's preliminary bid recap. WorldOil reported $82.7 million in high bids from 16 companies.

## Compared with March

The BBG2 sale on March 11 generated $46,976,423 in high bids for 25 blocks across about 141,000 acres, with 13 companies submitting 38 bids totaling $69,838,782. That bid reading was held in Kenner, Louisiana, with bids submitted at BOEM's New Orleans office.

## Louisiana's stake

The sources reviewed do not break down which blocks lie off Louisiana's coast. The law requires 30 Gulf lease sales in total, so the New Orleans area is likely to host a recurring series of bid readings.
