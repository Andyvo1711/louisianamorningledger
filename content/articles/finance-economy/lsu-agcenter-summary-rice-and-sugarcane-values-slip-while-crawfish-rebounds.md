Louisiana's 2025 agriculture summary from the LSU AgCenter shows contrasting fortunes. Rice yields improved modestly, but lower prices cut the industry's farm-gate value by about 15%. Sugarcane acreage and output edged up, but lower raw sugar prices pulled total value down by more than 5%. Farm-raised crawfish value rose more than 36% after yields rebounded.

## Costs and labor

AgCenter budget estimates put crawfish-only costs about 40% higher since 2014, and rice-crawfish producers are projected to lose roughly $88 per acre on the crawfish portion. The Farm Bureau notes these are engineering-based budgets, not producer surveys. Processors have also lost access to H-2B visas after a January rule limited supplemental visas to start dates after January 1.

## Federal aid gaps

Crawfish producers are excluded from the $11 billion Farmer Bridge Assistance Program. Crawfish contributes more than $300 million to the state's economy each year, according to the Department of Wildlife and Fisheries.

The sources reviewed contain no 2026 harvest forecast.
