Louisiana homeowners insurance has become a little cheaper and easier to find, according to an August 3 Times-Picayune piece, and state data show annual premium increases are essentially flat so far in 2026, versus 4.6% in 2025 and 6.6% in 2024. Another source cites 4.4% for 2025.

## Rate filings

The average homeowners rate change has dropped from a 14% increase in 2023 to 0.1% so far in 2026, according to state data. Of 16 homeowners rate filings finalized this year, nine reduced rates, four increased them and three left rates unchanged. State Farm, the largest homeowners insurer in Louisiana, was approved for a 9.7% average increase, one report said.

## Citizens

Citizens, the insurer of last resort, still has a stubbornly high policy count, and a waiver of its 10% surcharge took effect January 1 and lasts three years. Insurance Commissioner Tim Temple said in June that homeowners still struggle to find affordable coverage, especially along the coast.
