Louisiana Economic Development finalized a $1.2 million FastSites investment that will prime 75 acres at the Franklinton Industrial Park in Washington Parish, and planned to begin accepting applications for the next round of FastSites in October 2026.

## What FastSites does

The program prepares industrial sites so they are ready for companies, a response to the pace of recent project announcements. LED earlier selected 19 FastSites in what it called a historic state investment, and GNO Inc. noted in June that Louisiana leads the nation in REDI sites.

## What to watch

The release reviewed does not list the criteria for the next round or the total funding available, so interested parishes should check LED's site.
