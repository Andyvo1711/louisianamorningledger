Entergy Louisiana issued $1 billion in bonds with 10-year and 30-year maturities at an average rate below 6%, according to coverage of Entergy's November 2 third-quarter earnings call.

## Why the company is borrowing

Entergy is building new power plants for large customers, including ten connected to Meta's Richland Parish data center. Industrial sales rose 4.9% in the quarter, and management cited data-center interest in the range of 7 to 12 gigawatts. Meta has said it will cover construction costs rather than ratepayers.

## Rates and regulators

The sources reviewed do not say how the bond proceeds will be allocated among projects or how the issuance might affect customer rates.
