Louisiana's seasonally adjusted unemployment rate was 4.0% in September 2026, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on October 20. The figure is preliminary. The Center Square reported that the rate was down from 4.2% in both August and a year earlier.

## How the rate compares

Earlier in the year, USAFacts reported a 4.4% rate for June, and FRED listed 4.2% for December 2025. The Center Square notes that payroll counts and unemployment figures come from separate surveys that measure different things.

## What to watch

Parish-level rates vary widely. May data showed county rates ranging from 3.1% to 13.2%.

The Louisiana Workforce Commission's monthly releases carry the official state detail.
