Louisiana added about 36,000 payroll jobs over the past year, with construction accounting for more than half of the gain, according to preliminary figures from Louisiana Works reported by The Center Square.

## The details

Construction added 18,800 jobs compared with September 2025. Over the month, employers added 300 jobs from August, including 200 in the private sector. Seasonally adjusted nonfarm employment reached 2,024,200 in September, which the agency identified as a record high.

## Why construction

The state is in the middle of a wave of industrial projects, including LNG export terminals, a Hyundai-POSCO steel mill in Ascension Parish and data-center campuses in north Louisiana, which have raised demand for building trades.

The figures come from Louisiana Works rather than directly from the Louisiana Workforce Commission and are preliminary.
