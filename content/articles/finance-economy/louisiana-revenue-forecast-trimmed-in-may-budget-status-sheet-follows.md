Louisiana's state budget year that began July 1 is built on a trimmed revenue forecast. The Revenue Estimating Conference on May 8 lowered the current-year general fund estimate from $12,507.4 million to $12,394.5 million, a decline of $112.9 million, according to the legislature's records.

## FY27 numbers

The Public Affairs Research Council reported that the changes reduced the general fund money available for 2026-27 by $104 million, and said the forecast for next year went from $12.62 billion to $12.51 billion. A July 23 status sheet from the Joint Legislative Committee on the Budget lists $12,512.6 million in total available general fund revenue for FY27, tied to the May conference.

## What came after

A Revenue Estimating Conference was set for September 21, but its agenda listed the Unemployment Compensation Fund projection and a review of dedicated funds, not a general fund revision. The sources reviewed do not report what the meeting concluded.
