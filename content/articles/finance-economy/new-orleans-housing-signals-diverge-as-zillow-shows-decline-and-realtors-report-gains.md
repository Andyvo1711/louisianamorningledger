Different data sources paint different pictures of the New Orleans housing market in 2026.

## Zillow

Zillow data through August put the typical New Orleans home value at about $243,781, down roughly 1.9% over the year and up 0.1% from the prior month.

## Realtors and Redfin

The New Orleans Metropolitan Association of Realtors reported that, in the first half of 2026, the number of sales across the 10-parish region rose nearly 7% from a year earlier. In Orleans Parish, closed sales rose 5% and the median home sale price increased 2% to $285,000. Redfin, via a Stacker article from May, reported a median sale price of $304,154, up 6.7% from a year earlier. Biz New Orleans described sales as slowing while prices rise.

## Why the differences

The sources use different methodologies and time windows. Realtor.com's 2026 forecast projected metro sales falling 4.4% while prices rise 5.8%. The sources reviewed contain no 2026 rent data.
